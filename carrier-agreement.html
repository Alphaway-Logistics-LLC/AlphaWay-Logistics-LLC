Agreement terms

Alphaway Logistics will provide dispatch coordination services, including load sourcing, broker communication, rate negotiation, route support, and paperwork assistance. The carrier remains responsible for operating authority, insurance, safety compliance, equipment, drivers, cargo, and all obligations required by law.

The carrier authorizes Alphaway Logistics to communicate with brokers and shippers on the carrier's behalf for the purpose of arranging transportation. Rates, loads, and trip decisions remain subject to the carrier's approval.

Either party may end this agreement by written notice. Any fees, payment terms, and service details separately agreed in writing remain in effect for services already performed.
