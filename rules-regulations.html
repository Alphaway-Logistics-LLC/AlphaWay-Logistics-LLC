Driver rules and regulations.

A practical compliance guide for carriers working with Alphaway Logistics.

Important: This page is general information, not legal advice. Requirements can change and depend on your vehicle, cargo, operations, route, and carrier status. Confirm your obligations with FMCSA, Colorado/CDOT, your insurer, and a qualified transportation-compliance professional.

01 / AUTHORITY

Operating authority

Maintain the registrations and authority required for your operation before accepting freight. Keep your USDOT and MC information current and ensure your carrier profile is accurate.

02 / INSURANCE

Insurance and filings

Carry the insurance required for your operation and cargo. Keep certificates and filings current, and notify the appropriate parties before a policy changes or lapses.

03 / HOURS OF SERVICE

Hours of service

Follow applicable FMCSA hours-of-service limits, rest requirements, and duty-status rules. Do not drive when fatigued, and plan legal parking and breaks before the load moves.

04 / ELD

Logs and electronic devices

Use a compliant electronic logging device when required, keep records accurate, and be prepared to transfer or display records during an inspection. Never edit or falsify logs.

05 / SAFETY

Vehicle inspections

Complete required pre-trip and post-trip inspections, address safety defects promptly, and keep inspection, repair, and maintenance records available as required.

06 / CARGO

Cargo securement

Load, distribute, and secure cargo according to applicable rules. Recheck securement during the trip and stop if the load shifts, leaks, or becomes unsafe.

07 / DRUG & ALCOHOL

Testing and qualification

Follow applicable DOT drug and alcohol testing, Clearinghouse, medical qualification, and driver-training requirements. Keep required records and certifications current.

08 / ROADWAY

Permits and route limits

Verify weight, height, length, escort, toll, chain, weather, and state-specific requirements for every route. Colorado and other states may impose additional rules.

Check the official sources

Use these government resources for current requirements and state-specific guidance. When an official rule conflicts with this summary, the official rule controls.
